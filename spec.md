THE MARCHAOS BRAND CODEX

IDENTITY
 Name: Marchaos
 Real Name: Marcos (rarely referenced, only in poetic whispers)
 Tagline: "The drop was foretold."
 Genre: Dark Progressive House with ancestral + mystic undertones
 Core Archetype: The Vessel — not a DJ, but a conduit for fate-bound rhythm
Origin Story:
 He was called "Gypsy" his entire life by his family. One day, a fortune teller in Buenos Aires told him he would become the international DJ of the gypsy frequency. Not long after, fate aligned. A name emerged. A set was played. A soul split: Marcos remained behind. Marchaos walked forward.

AESTHETIC UNIVERSE
Symbols & Motifs:
Alchemical symbols (especially earth, transformation, entropy)


UFOs, alien transmission schematics


Skeletons as rhythm incarnate


Candles, fog, tiled Buenos Aires alleyways


Match strike = signature sonic motif


Color Palette:
Sepia-infused blacks, smoky browns, muted obsidian


Accents: ritual red (#D0011B), spectral violet (#7F00FF), copper glow


Core Visual Concept:
Imagine a dark progressive Woodstock held on a Buenos Aires rooftop during a blackout. Posters printed by the void. Cloaks optional.

SOUND + SONIC PHILOSOPHY
Described As:
"Like the moment before a storm became a nightclub."


"If Borges DJ'd in Berlin with ghost samples and a broken heart."


"Basslines deep enough to bury secrets in."


Sacred Sounds & Textures:
Deep sub-bass, 124-128 BPM


Filtered bandoneon (used texturally, not melodically)


Wind textures, field recordings, match strikes


Whispered/reversed Spanish phrases


Ghost radio interference, tape hiss


Sparse, minor-key melodic refrains


What Doesn’t Belong:
Bright synths, happy chords


Pop vocals


EDM-style build-and-drop cycles


Humor samples (his humor is structural, not overt)



VOICE & PERSONALITY
